The mark is an Orbit

It's the same instrument the Mini and the Pill show: a ring of rays whose length follows an episode's loudness. The played third is in the accent, with a head dot where playback is. The rest fades as it goes, so the brightest point is always the one just played.

Do

  • Keep the gap, the head dot and the played third where they are.
  • Leave clear space of a quarter of its width.
  • Light mark on light grounds, dark on dark.

Don't

  • Rotate it, fill the ring or even out the rays.
  • Recolour the played third anything but the accent.
  • Add shadow, glass, gradients or a wordmark inside the tile.

Wordmark candidates

Six calm, futuristic faces, all set light with open tracking and all in ink. The accent stays with the mark. The wordmark is for the README, the site, the DMG window and the About panel. Inside the app, the interface stays in Hanken Grotesk. Click one to try it at the top of the page.

Words, not widgets

A choice is a row of words. A control is a line glyph or a ring. The app has no cards, no switches and no filled buttons. Everything below works.

Transport

A 1px ring that turns accent on hover. Glyphs use a 1.1px stroke and no fill.

Word group

Faint, dim on hover, full ink when chosen.

Rows

Playing is accent. Heard drops to half strength.

No box, just an underline that turns accent when you type.

Confirmed in words, never in a dialog. Red only while you hover.

The room is quiet.

Zenpod talks like a calm person in the room with you. It's short, plain and specific. It says what happened, then what you can do.

  1. Say it like a person, not a system: The room is quiet.
  2. Say what happened, then what to do: No public feed was found for this show, so it can't be played here. If it has one, paste its address below.
  3. Be exact: 3 new shows, 2 you already had.
  4. Never blame, never apologise: Their feeds may be private or gone.
  5. Name the machinery only when you have to, and say where to find it: An OPML file, from Overcast, Pocket Casts or most other podcast apps.
  6. Use sentence case and British spelling. No exclamation marks, no emoji.
